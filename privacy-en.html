Privacy policy

Updated: November 2026

Who we are

WayIn provides consulting on building footballers' careers. We operate from Ukraine. Contact for personal data questions: welcome@wayin.football.

What data we collect

Only what you enter in the application form: the player's name and year of birth, position, current club and country, video link, contact person, email or phone.

Why

To reply to your application, assess the player's level and offer a consultation. We don't use the data for advertising and never sell it.

Legal basis

Your consent, given in the form, under the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection" and, for users in the EU, the GDPR. If the player is under 18, consent is given by their legal guardian.

Who we share it with

We share data with clubs and academies only with your separate consent. To deliver applications we use the Web3Forms form service and an email provider, which receive the data for that technical purpose only.

How long we keep it

For the duration of our cooperation and up to 12 months after the last contact. After that the data is deleted.

Your rights

You can find out what data we hold, correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to welcome@wayin.football. You also have the right to contact the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.
