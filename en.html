Four barriers on the way to senior football

01

Mindset

Losing your status as the leader, a coach with no time to forgive mistakes, pressure for results in every match.
02

Physical and tactical

Duels with grown men, faster decision-making, tactical discipline instead of individual tricks.
03

Money and surroundings

A first contract and the lure of the high life, a new dressing room full of 30-year-old professionals.
04

The system

A gap between U19 and the first team. Without an intermediate step — a loan or a lower-level club — very few break through.

We prepare players for all four — not just find them a club.

Rules we don't break

There's no shortage of "agents" promising Europe for an upfront fee. We work differently — and say so openly.

  • No contract guarantees
    The club makes the decision. We're responsible for the quality of preparation and access, not for someone else's choice.
  • FIFA rules on minors
    International transfers under 18 are restricted. We explain to families which exceptions exist and whether any apply to them.
  • Written parental consent
    With minors, we work only with the written consent of their legal guardians and in their presence.
  • Transparent fees
    A fixed consulting fee, known in advance. No percentage of contracts and no payments for a "guaranteed trial".
  • Trials are free
    Trials at partner clubs are free for the player. If anyone asks for money for a trial in our name, please tell us.
  • Open about conflicts of interest
    If we have a connection to a club or academy under discussion, you'll be the first to know.
  • Your child's data is protected
    We process personal data in line with Ukrainian law and the GDPR and never share it with clubs without your consent.

Questions and answers

Do you guarantee a contract with a European club?
No. Anyone who guarantees one is misleading you. We guarantee an honest assessment, quality preparation and access to clubs at the right level.
My son is 15. Can he join a European club?
It depends on the family's situation. FIFA rules restrict international transfers under 18, but there are exceptions — for example, if the family already lives in the club's country for reasons unrelated to football. We'll look at your case in a consultation.
How much does it cost?
The cost depends on the services you need, from a one-off assessment of the player's level to support throughout a season. We give an exact price after the first conversation, once we understand what your player needs. The fee is fixed, agreed in advance and doesn't depend on whether the player signs a contract.
Are you agents?
No. We don't represent players in negotiations with clubs. When it comes to a contract, the player chooses a licensed FIFA agent or lawyer — we'll explain what to look out for.
Does my son need to speak the language of the country where he'll play?
Not at the start. But language is part of the plan from month one: in the dressing room and in negotiations it matters as much as technique.
